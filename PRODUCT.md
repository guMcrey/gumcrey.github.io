# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Players and app-store reviewers opening a public privacy-policy URL, often on a phone. The existing site serves Brazilian Portuguese and English readers. Tiro Total is intended for players aged 13 and older.

## Product Purpose

Publish readable, app-specific privacy policies on GitHub Pages. Tiro Total uses its own `/tiro-total/privacy/` path. The existing Photo Luck / Pomar Zen policy remains at the site root.

## Brand Personality

Plain, calm, precise. Continue the established document-oriented presentation rather than introducing a game landing page.

## Anti-references

Promotional landing pages, decorative animation, dense legal walls without navigation, consent banners on a page that has no advertising or analytics scripts.

## Design Principles

- Identify the application, developer and revision clearly.
- Cover Google AdMob in ordinary policy language, as requested by the user, with advertising provisions applying to versions and features that offer ads. Do not assert that a particular SDK or control is already present in a version without it.
- Keep Portuguese and English information equivalent.
- Reuse the existing visual system and preserve the established app policies.
- Let readers find data, advertising, rights and contact information without logging in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic headings, a skip link, keyboard-visible focus, readable line lengths, responsive navigation and print support. No external fonts or scripts are needed.
