At a glance

  • Tiro Total does not require an account. Progress and preferences are saved on your device.

  • Rewarded ads are optional. You can revisit applicable privacy choices.

Features and privacy choices

The availability of features and ads depends on the game version, your region, applicable age and privacy choices. The advertising provisions apply when you use a version that offers ads. The sections below explain the data used, its purposes and the available controls.

01About this policy

This policy applies to the Tiro Total Android game, identified by com.tirototal.app, and this privacy page. The developer responsible for processing carried out by the game is gumeracy. You can contact us at gumeracy@gmail.com.

It explains how the game handles information, including advertising and third-party services where available. Versions made available on other platforms or with different services will disclose their practices before release. This policy is specific to Tiro Total; the developer's other applications have their own policy URLs.

02Information used

Game data
Level progress, coins and other virtual resources, upgrades, unlocked weapons and items, appearance choices, daily check-in and reward records, and timestamps used to restore resources. These records let you continue playing and calculate rewards on your device.
Preferences
Language, audio, haptic feedback where available, and other game settings are stored locally to keep your choices between sessions.
Voluntary contact
If you send us an email, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to share. We use it to respond, provide support and handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents unnecessarily.
Visiting this page
This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its privacy statement. We do not add ads, visitor analytics, our own cookies or tracking scripts to this page.

The game does not require registration, does not synchronize progress with a developer-operated server and does not automatically send local game records to us. You do not need to provide your name or email address to play. Age or guardian-authorization information may be requested where necessary to apply protections for minors and advertising choices.

Android may include app data in system backups or transfers between devices, depending on system and user settings. This processing is handled by the operating system or backup provider, rather than a developer-operated cloud service.

03Permissions

Internet and connectivity
The app declares access to the internet and network and Wi-Fi connection status. These permissions allow ads to load and information needed for privacy choices to be obtained in versions that offer advertising. Game progress is saved on your device and does not require a developer-operated server.
Local storage
The game saves data in its private storage area on your device. This does not require access to your photos or other apps' files.
Advertising identifier
In versions with advertising, Google AdMob services may use the advertising identifier, app set identifier and, where applicable, other device or account identifiers to provide and measure ads and prevent fraud. Use depends on applicable consent, age, region and settings. Android settings allow you to reset or delete the advertising identifier, depending on the system version.

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, messages, photo library or precise location. Advertising services may estimate an approximate location from your IP address without location permission.

04Ads and third-party services

Google AdMob and rewarded ads

Tiro Total may offer ads through Google AdMob to fund the game. Formats may include rewarded ads, interstitials and banners, depending on the features of the version you use.

Rewarded ads are optional. Before you choose to watch, the game explains the benefit, such as coins, an increased reward, an item or continuing a game. Rewards depend on completing the stated viewing conditions. Closing an ad before meeting those conditions or a loading failure may prevent the reward from being granted. You do not need to click the ad, install another app or buy anything to receive the reward. Declining that ad does not prevent access to basic gameplay or remove rewards already earned. Test versions may use test ads without advertising revenue.

According to Google's documentation, its advertising service collects and shares data to display and measure ads, analyze their operation and prevent fraud. In versions that use the service, this data may include:

  • IP address and approximate location estimated from it.
  • Advertising and app set identifiers and, where applicable, other device or account identifiers.
  • Device, operating-system, app and connectivity information needed for ads to function.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, impressions, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as launch time, errors, hangs and energy usage.
  • Consent choices and records needed to apply them or confirm delivery of rewards.

The data actually used depends on the service version, region, applicable age and privacy settings. Non-personalized ads may also process data for delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

See the Google Privacy Policy, information about Google advertising, data use in partner apps, and the advertising service's data disclosure. If we add other ad networks, independent usage analytics or new forms of collection, we will identify those services and update this policy before enabling them.

05Consent and choices

In versions with advertising, required information and consent requests are presented before processing that depends on consent. Where applicable, Google's privacy tools, including the User Messaging Platform (UMP), offer choices about personalized advertising and other data uses before ads are requested.

Versions with ads provide a visible entry point to revisit privacy choices whenever required. The availability of personalized, non-personalized or limited ads depends on your choices, age, region and the service's rules. You can revisit options through the game's ad privacy control, where available. We do not carry out processing that requires consent without that consent.

Reading this policy, installing or continuing to use the game does not replace specific consent where required. Refusing or withdrawing consent may affect the availability of ads and optional rewards; it does not prevent access to basic gameplay.

You can also manage permissions and advertising identifiers in Android settings and use Google's ad controls, where available. These controls do not guarantee deletion of previously processed data and do not replace in-app choices.

Where applicable law treats certain advertising uses as a sale or sharing of data, versions engaging in those uses provide the required notices and opt-out mechanisms. We do not sell your local progress records.

06Sharing and security

We do not automatically send local progress records to the developer or an ad network. This is separate from data processed by the advertising service, such as ad interactions and technical information. If you contact us, your email provider and ours process the communication to deliver and store it under their policies.

In versions with AdMob, Google and the partners involved in ad delivery may process data for the purposes and choices described in this policy and Google's information. New advertising-mediation services will be identified before they are enabled. We may also disclose information we hold where necessary to meet legal obligations, respond to a valid authority request or protect rights and safety, within the limits of the law.

Email, hosting, backup and advertising services may process data in countries other than where you live. Where required, these transfers must comply with the conditions and safeguards provided by applicable law and the respective providers' policies.

We limit access to information we receive to the purposes described and take reasonable measures to protect it. According to Google's documentation, data collected by its advertising service is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of storage or transmission offers absolute security.

07Retention and deletion

Local game data: remains on your device until deleted. You can remove it through Android settings, under Apps → Tiro Total → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. This may permanently erase progress, resources and unlocked items.

Backups: clearing data or uninstalling the app may not remove copies already kept by the system. Also manage backup copies and restore settings in Android or with your backup provider. We do not offer progress recovery through a developer account.

Support and privacy messages: we retain information for as long as needed to resolve your request and, where necessary, meet legal obligations or protect rights. You can request deletion through the email provided, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

Third-party data: hosting, email, backup and advertising records follow the respective providers' retention periods and criteria. We do not control those periods or promise to erase records outside our possession. Deleting the game or withdrawing consent does not automatically erase data previously processed by these services; also use their privacy channels.

Because the game does not create user accounts, there is no game account to delete. Contact us to request deletion of data received directly by the developer.

08Your rights

Under applicable law, including Brazil's LGPD and the GDPR where relevant, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, portability, information about sharing, objection or restriction of processing, and withdrawal of consent where it is the basis used. Each right is subject to the conditions and exceptions provided by law.

We process information to provide requested features, respond to your messages, protect the service and meet legal obligations, using the applicable legal basis for each case. For advertising that depends on consent, we request it before processing; other permitted uses depend on the relevant legal conditions and disclosed choices.

To exercise your rights over data processed by the developer, write to gumeracy@gmail.com. We may request only the information necessary to locate your request and verify its legitimacy. Because we do not automatically receive local game records, you may need to delete them directly on your device.

For information processed by Google or other providers, also use the channels listed in their policies. You can complain to the competent data protection authority; in Brazil, see information from the National Data Protection Agency (ANPD).

09Protection of minors

Tiro Total is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. This does not remove the protections provided for teenagers. An app-store age rating is not consent to data processing.

If the law in your region requires a parent or guardian's authorization to use the service or for certain processing, that authorization is required even if you are already 13. The local age for consenting to data processing may be higher than 13.

In versions with advertising, we do not request personalized ads for users identified as under 18. We apply age restrictions and consent mechanisms required by law and the advertising service. Features that depend on unavailable authorization may have limited or disabled advertising.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we received a child's data without required authorization, contact us by email so we can investigate, stop improper processing and delete information under our control where appropriate.

Material changes will be communicated in the app or through other appropriate means, and new services or forms of collection will have the necessary notices and consent requests before they are enabled.
