At a glance

  • Photos from the camera feature are processed on your device and are not sent to the developer.
  • Game progress stays on your device. Google advertising services process their own data.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You can revisit applicable privacy choices.

Features depend on your version

Photo Luck and Pomar Zen share the identifier com.photoluck.app. The camera feature belongs to photography versions of Photo Luck. The Pomar Zen game update does not request camera access or process photos. The sections below explain which version each practice applies to.

01About this policy

This policy covers Photo Luck and its update to the Pomar Zen game, identified by com.photoluck.app. The developer responsible for the application is gumeracy. For privacy questions, contact gumeracy@gmail.com.

The camera and attribution information preserves the purposes stated in Photo Luck’s previous policy, published on March 3, 2026. The information about progress, rewarded ads and Google AdMob describes the Pomar Zen update. Check the features of the version you use.

02Camera and photographs

Photo Luck versions with photography features

Camera permission allows you to take photos and use the application’s photography features. As stated in the previous policy:

  • Local processing: images are used for display or processing on the device itself.
  • No saving to your album: the application does not save these photos to your phone’s permanent photo album.
  • No uploading of photos: the developer does not receive the images; the application does not send them to servers or third parties.

The Pomar Zen update does not use this feature or request camera permission. In versions that use it, you can deny or revoke permission in Android settings; the photography feature will stop working.

03Permissions and identifiers

Advertising identifier (GAID)
Photo Luck’s previous policy states that it is used for app analytics and installation-source attribution. In the Pomar Zen update, Google AdMob services may use the advertising identifier, app set identifier and, where applicable, other device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Android provides controls to reset or delete the advertising identifier.
Internet and connection status
In Pomar Zen, these allow ads to load and privacy-choice information to be obtained. Game resources are bundled with the application; the first level and already unlocked levels can be played without loading new ads.
Vibration
In Pomar Zen, this provides haptic feedback for game actions. You can turn it off in game settings.
Local storage
The game saves progress in its private storage area on your device. This does not require access to your photos or other applications’ files.

The game does not request precise location, microphone, contacts or photo-album access. Network data such as your IP address can allow the advertising service to estimate an approximate location even without location permission.

04Local data and retention

In Pomar Zen, unlocked levels, stars, medals, preferences, the current round and reward records stay in local storage. We do not require an account or maintain a server to synchronize game progress.

Local records remain until you delete them in game settings, clear the application’s data in Android or uninstall it. These actions may permanently erase progress and unlocked levels; we do not offer account recovery or cloud synchronization.

Photos in photography versions follow the conditions in the camera section. Data processed by third-party services follows the purposes and retention periods stated by those services, which are separate from local game records.

05Ads and third-party services

Pomar Zen update with Google AdMob

We use optional rewarded ads for two features:

  • Unlock a level: completing an ad permanently opens the selected level on this device.
  • Continue with five moves: completing an ad adds five moves to a lost round, at most once per round.

The first level and already unlocked levels remain available without viewing new ads. Closing an ad before meeting its conditions does not grant that ad’s reward. The ad presents its duration and completion conditions. You do not need to click the ad or purchase a product to receive the reward. Test builds use test ads and earn no advertising revenue.

According to Google’s documentation, its advertising SDK collects and shares data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as startup time, crashes and energy use.
  • Device and, where applicable, account identifiers, such as the advertising identifier and app set identifier.

Collection may vary depending on consent, region, settings and the features used. See Google’s Privacy Policy, its information about Google advertising and the SDK’s data disclosure to learn how these data are processed.

Photo Luck’s previous policy also describes possible analytics or attribution services that use GAID to measure usage and installation sources. That description applies to previous versions; the current game does not include a separate analytics or attribution SDK beyond the advertising services described here.

06Your choices and rights

Where applicable, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads are requested. To revisit available choices in the game, open Settings → Privacy & local data → Ad privacy choices. Availability depends on your region and consent status.

You can also manage app permissions and advertising-identifier controls in Android settings. Declining personalized advertising may change which ads are available. Declining a rewarded ad does not prevent you from playing the first level or already unlocked levels.

Under applicable law, including Brazil’s LGPD where relevant, you may request information about the processing of your data and exercise rights such as access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent, where available. Send requests to gumeracy@gmail.com. We may ask for the minimum information needed to identify the request and respond securely.

Deleting local game records does not automatically delete data Google has already processed. For data and choices managed by Google, also consult the channels and controls described in its privacy policy.

07Security and external processing

The application does not send your photos or game progress to a developer server. Advertising services connect to Google systems; according to Google’s documentation, data collected by its SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Processing by Google services may take place in other countries under the conditions and safeguards described in Google’s policy. We do not sell your local game records. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

08Application audience

Pomar Zen is designed for adult players aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. This description applies to the game and does not retroactively change the rating of previous photography versions.

If you believe the developer has received a child’s data in connection with the application, please contact us so that we can assess the situation and take appropriate action.
